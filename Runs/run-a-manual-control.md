# Run A — Manual Control Process

## Experiment

**AI Automation Test #1 — Brand Awareness & Market Entry**

## Run Configuration

**Run:** A
**Configuration:** Manual Control Process
**Purpose:** Establish the human-operated baseline against which the AI-enabled processes could be compared.

---

## Process Objective

Run A represents the conventional human-led approach to the defined marketing process:

> **Building brand awareness through digital integration**

The process was executed as a manual control configuration using the defined Brand Awareness & Market Entry Campaign requirements and reference dataset.

The purpose was not to demonstrate the best possible marketing process.

The purpose was to establish a reference point for:

* operational workload;
* human involvement;
* process sequence;
* decision requirements;
* time consumption;
* and quality and control considerations.

---

## Operating Model

Under the manual control configuration, the process relied primarily on human effort to:

1. interpret the campaign requirements;
2. review and analyse the available information;
3. determine strategic direction;
4. develop campaign decisions;
5. produce or prepare required outputs;
6. review the resulting work;
7. identify potential risks or weaknesses;
8. make final decisions; and
9. incorporate relevant learning into subsequent decisions.

No deliberate AI-based process automation was applied as part of the control configuration.

---

## Human Responsibility

Human responsibility remained primary throughout the process.

The human operator was responsible for:

* strategic interpretation;
* analysis;
* decision-making;
* creative judgement;
* quality control;
* risk identification;
* approval;
* and accountability for the resulting work.

This established the reference condition against which the effect of introducing AI could later be examined.

---

## Process Time

The manual control process required approximately:

> **464 minutes**

across the measured stages.

This figure represents the defined experimental process rather than a claim about the amount of time required for every real-world brand awareness campaign.

The figure establishes the baseline for comparison with Runs B and C.

---

## Capacity Characteristics

The manual configuration required substantial human involvement across both strategic and operational activities.

This meant that human capacity was consumed not only by decisions requiring expertise, but also by activities involving:

* information processing;
* preparation;
* coordination;
* repetitive analysis;
* production;
* checking;
* and process administration.

This distinction became important when identifying opportunities for AI integration in Run B.

---

## Quality and Control

The manual configuration provided a high degree of direct human control because the process was primarily human-operated.

However, manual operation does not automatically eliminate risk.

Potential weaknesses included:

* human error;
* inconsistent execution;
* repetitive workload;
* cognitive fatigue;
* limited processing capacity;
* and the possibility of important information being overlooked.

These characteristics were considered when evaluating the potential benefits and risks of AI integration.

---

## Baseline Strengths

The manual process provided several inherent strengths:

* direct human judgement;
* clear human accountability;
* contextual interpretation;
* flexibility when encountering ambiguity;
* and direct oversight of consequential decisions.

These strengths were treated as characteristics that an AI-enabled process should preserve rather than automatically replace.

---

## Baseline Weaknesses

The process also demonstrated potential areas of inefficiency:

* high human time requirements;
* significant operational workload;
* repeated information-processing activities;
* limited scalability of purely human execution;
* and the opportunity cost of using skilled human capacity for lower-value operational work.

These weaknesses informed the design of Run B.

---

## Role in the Experiment

Run A served as the **control condition**.

Its purpose was to provide a consistent reference against which the AI-enabled configurations could be assessed.

The key question arising from Run A was:

> **Which parts of this process genuinely require human judgement, and which parts consume human capacity without requiring equivalent human involvement?**

That question became the basis for the AI-engineering work undertaken in Run B.

---

## Evidence Classification

Run A represents the **manual control configuration** within the controlled experiment.

The approximately **464-minute** process time provides the baseline measurement for the subsequent comparative runs.

The baseline should not be interpreted as a universal benchmark for marketing processes outside the defined experimental conditions.
