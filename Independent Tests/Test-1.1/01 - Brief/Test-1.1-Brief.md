# Test 1.1 Brief

## Test

AI-Assisted Lead Generation Efficiency

## Purpose

To determine whether integrating AI into an existing human-led lead-generation process reduces the total time required to produce a lead.

## Problem

AI is frequently evaluated as a standalone tool or isolated task rather than as part of an end-to-end human workflow.

This test evaluates AI as an integrated resource within the existing process.

## Comparison

### Run A
Human-only lead-generation process.

### Run B
The same overall process with AI integrated into appropriate activities alongside human work.

## Primary Measure

Total end-to-end production time per lead.

## Secondary Observation

The amount of human capacity potentially recovered through AI integration.

## Scope

This test evaluates process efficiency.

It does not attempt to prove improved lead quality, conversion, response rate or commercial value.
