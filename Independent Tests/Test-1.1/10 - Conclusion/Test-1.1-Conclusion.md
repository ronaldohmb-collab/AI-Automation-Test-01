# Test 1.1 Conclusion

Test 1.1 supports the hypothesis that integrating AI into a human-led lead-generation process can improve end-to-end production efficiency.

The human-only process required 156.5 minutes per lead.

The integrated human + AI process required 136 minutes per lead.

This produced a saving of 20.5 minutes per lead and a 13.1% improvement in end-to-end efficiency.

The result demonstrates a measurable efficiency benefit from AI integration.

However, the test does not demonstrate improved lead quality or commercial performance.

The recovered human capacity therefore becomes the subject of the next research question: whether deliberately reinvesting that capacity into higher-value human activities can improve the quality of leads produced.
